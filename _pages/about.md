I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at MIT, working with <a href="https://evlab.mit.edu/">Evelina Fedorenko</a>. My research asks how the brain processes language across the world's linguistic diversity — spanning typologically diverse languages, from Indo-European to indigenous languages of the Amazon, and diverse speakers, including bilinguals, multilinguals, polyglots, and heritage speakers. I use neuroimaging methods combined with cross-cultural fieldwork to understand what is universal about language in the brain, and what varies across languages and speakers.

I completed my PhD in <a href="https://shbtprogram.harvard.edu/">Speech and Hearing Bioscience and Technology</a> at Harvard University, working with <a href="https://evlab.mit.edu/">Evelina Fedorenko</a> and <a href="http://tedlab.mit.edu/">Edward Gibson</a>. Before that, I did a Master's in the Cognitive Neuroscience of Language at the <a href="https://www.bcbl.eu/">Basque Center on Cognition, Brain and Language</a> in San Sebastián, Spain, and a B.Sc. in Psychology at the <a href="https://www.uab.cat/">Autonomous University of Barcelona</a>.
